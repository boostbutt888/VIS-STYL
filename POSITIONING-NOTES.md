# VIS positioning and claim review

Reviewed 19 October 2026. This is an internal editorial note, not part of the public landing page.

## Recommended lead

Integrated payments, controlled corporate staff benefits and practical operator workflows across compatible unattended machines.

The strongest story is the detail of the workflow: identify a member, apply machine/item/time/usage rules, administer their points, then see the usage in reports. Pair that with pre-kitting and dated refill records for the operator.

## What is and is not established against competitors

- Galaxis publicly advertises fleet health, stock and sales, service work, reporting, mobile operations, and membership/allocation/voucher/redemption through MARS. Broad claims that competitors lack operations, loyalty or member programmes would be inaccurate.
- Payrallel presents payment orchestration and explicitly links to Galaxis for unattended operations and MARS-related capabilities. These are connected competitor offerings, not evidence of two wholly independent feature sets.
- The reviewed Galaxis homepage advertises Android mobile access and says iOS is coming soon. VIS has an iPhone App Store listing. This is a dated, publicly observable channel distinction; it does not prove broader app superiority or current feature parity. The public page links to the VIS iPhone listing without making an exclusivity claim.
- STYL's material specifies detailed membership limits, SFTP/CSV wallet administration, pre-kitting and refill records. Those details are valuable to highlight. Their absence from the competitor pages reviewed does not establish that competitors cannot provide them.
- Galaxis's supported-platforms page includes MDB and VCCS-related models. Do not describe protocol support alone as exclusive to STYL.
- No independently verified evidence supports “only platform”, “competitors cannot”, lower costs, better uptime, faster performance or numerical ROI claims. None were added.

## Corrections made to the previous page

- Removed available-now claims for detailed technician service logging and parts tracking. The ecosystem deck identifies service logs as roadmap; parts tracking was not substantiated as a current capability by the new collateral.
- Kept member privileges and tier discounts as roadmap rather than live features.
- Replaced broad earning-rewards language with points allocations, member schemes and redemption controls actually described in the materials.
- Did not expose internal funding, named project commitments, licensing strategy, supplier discussions or commercial roadblocks from the internal slides.
- Did not publish the wallet deck's staff screenshots; the page uses conceptual interfaces and contains no staff records.
