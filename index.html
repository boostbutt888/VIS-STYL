Machine connectivity & configuration

Integration depth.
From legacy to smart.

Connect through the interface your machine supports, then configure the experience for the deployment.

Legacy operations

Pulse mode

Support basic vending operations through a pulse interface. Configure pulses, duration, intervals and pricing per selection.

Vending interfaces

MDB

Multi-Drop Bus support for compatible vending machines and their payment peripherals.

Additional machine protocols

VCCS / JVMA

Interface compatibility for supported machines using these vending communication standards.

Configure the customer experience.

For pulse deployments, customise selection order, display messages and items per page. Supported proprietary interfaces can show rich product images and descriptions.

Proprietary integrations can support separate cash and cashless pricing, plus monitoring of temperature, coin balance and fault codes.

Plan your rollout

A few useful
details.

Can we use our existing staff credentials?

Identification options include corporate access cards, operator-issued cards, QR codes and API integration, with supported MIFARE and HID card options. STYL can confirm compatibility with your credential format and deployment.

Can benefits be limited by product, location and time?

Yes. Membership controls include machine and item restrictions, validity periods, time-of-day access, and daily, weekly or monthly quantity and spending limits.

Which payment markets are covered?

The documented payment offering includes QR and card payments for Singapore and Malaysia. Philippines QR and card payments are listed as in development, with bespoke member schemes available. Ask STYL to confirm the current market and acquirer configuration for your rollout.

Are service logs and member-tier privileges available?

Technician service logs and member privileges, including tier-based discounts, are listed on the product roadmap. Ask STYL about current release status and project scope.

What should we bring to the first conversation?

Your machine models and interfaces, deployment markets, preferred payment methods, staff credential types and benefit rules. STYL can use these to discuss the configuration for your fleet.
